What working with us looks like.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Share read access to your fleet's telemetry and your rental contracts. Most robots already send what we need: hours, uptime, output and location.

  2. 02

    Underwrite

    We score each robot model, each customer and each contract, then make an offer backed by our capital partners.

Daedalus / Why now

Three things changed at the same time.

Robots now do paid work on real job sites. They're rented rather than sold. And the lenders who could fund them have no way to judge one. Daedalus sits where all three meet.

The overlap

Where the three meet.

  • 349,000new construction workers the US needs in 2026, so contractors are turning to robots.1
  • $50.7Byearly rate of US data-center construction spending in April 2026, a record.1
  • 99.97%accuracy of DEWALT's drilling robot across more than 230,000 holes on data-center jobs.4
  • $50Mraised by Formic in October 2026. Its lead investor named financing robots in real plants as a new bottleneck.5
Our edge

Money is easy to find. Robot data isn't.

Plenty of lenders fund machines, and robot makers know their own robots. Few can combine performance data from many robot makers with the capital to back it. That combination is the business.
