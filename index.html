Why now

Three things changed at the same time.

Robots now do paid work on real job sites. They are rented rather than sold. And the lenders who could fund them have no way to judge one. Daedalus sits where all three meet.

The shift

Where the three meet.

01 / Work

Robots do paid work.

Construction robots are already drilling, printing layout and tying rebar on commercial projects. The work can be measured.

02 / Ownership

Rental is the default.

Contractors prefer monthly rental because robots are new, service matters and projects end. That creates a financing need.

03 / Credit

Lenders cannot see the asset.

Traditional equipment lenders do not know whether a robot is working, idle, broken or missing. Telemetry answers that.

Position

Money is easy to find. Robot data is not.

Plenty of lenders fund machines, and robot makers know their own robots. Few can combine performance data from many robot makers with the capital to back it. That combination is the business.
