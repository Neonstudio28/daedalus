Privacy.

Last updated 22 November 2026

This page explains what the Daedalus Systems website collects and what we do with it. It covers this website only. Telemetry, rental contracts and customer details shared during a financing deal are covered by the agreements for that deal.

What this site collects

  • The site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and doesn't identify you. It records the page you view, the site that referred you, your browser, operating system, device type and country, and we only see those as totals.
  • The contact form doesn't send anything. It builds a note in your browser for you to copy into your own email. Nothing you type in it reaches us unless you send that email yourself.
  • Our hosting provider may record standard request details, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you load, to deliver and secure the site.

Services the site loads

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr. Your browser contacts those services directly, so they receive your IP address and browser details under their own policies: Google Fonts and jsDelivr.

The sources we cite link to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your name, your email address and whatever you include, such as your company, your role and details about your fleet. We use it to reply, to work out whether we can finance your fleet, and to keep a record of our conversation.

We share it only with people who help us respond or assess a deal. That includes our capital partners when you ask us to pursue financing, and advisers or authorities when the law requires it. We don't sell personal information.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep emails for as long as we need them for the reasons above, or as long as the law requires. You can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold about you. To ask, email us at hello@daedalus.tech.
